Accessibility information

  • We’re expecting high footfall at this event; please expect large crowds, with peak hours from 11am–2pm. Please consider this when planning to attend.
  • Ear plugs: collect from stewards or security at entry and exit points, or ask any member of staff (staff will be wearing cyan blue t-shirts).
  • Physical access: the Curzon Building is wheelchair accessible.
  • Bringing a carer or support person: if you’d like to be accompanied by a support worker, family member or friend, please email disability@bcu.ac.uk in advance – we’ll need their name, their relationship to you, and confirmation of who they’ll be accompanying, so we can add them to the guest list and make sure they’re able to get through the entry checks on the day. If you’d like the University to arrange a support worker for you instead, please include this in your email.
  • Outdoor activities: the mini petting zoo is wheelchair accessible, but the Meltdown inflatable activity and rock climbing wall are not.
  • Quiet Room: a designated room is located in C001. Access it through the Library – enter the library, turn immediately right and go towards the end of the room to find C001. If you need a silent room to relax, please visit our Calm Space (C144) on the first floor.

How do I take part?

  1. Check our society events (workshops, meet-ups, classes or socials) on our What's On page
  2. Give it a Go - attend sessions and meet other members. No need to buy a membership.
  3. Find their social media channels. All our groups are student-led, the best way to find out about events could be on their social media.
